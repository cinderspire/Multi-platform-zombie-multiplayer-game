## 📖 **SYSTEM INTEGRATION**

All systems are **fully integrated** with each other:

```
HitMarkerSystem → DamageSystem → ComboSystem → MilestoneSystem → TitleSystem
      ↓              ↓              ↓              ↓              ↓
DamageNumbers  KillcamSystem  MatchHistory  LoginBonus  PlayerProfile
```

**Example Flow**:
1. Player shoots zombie (WeaponFiringSystem)
2. Damage calculated (DamageSystem)
3. Hit marker shown (HitMarkerSystem)
4. Damage number floats (DamageNumberSystem)
5. Kill registered (CombatSystem)
6. Combo updated (ComboSystem)
7. Milestone progress (MilestoneSystem)
8. Match history recorded (MatchHistorySystem)
